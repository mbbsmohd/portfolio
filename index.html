How I work

End-to-end, without losing the real problem.

My approach combines research discipline, product thinking and technical awareness.

Discover

Understand users, stakeholders, business constraints and the existing workflow. Use interviews, observation, data and documentation where available.

Define

Turn observations into a clear problem statement, user needs, priorities and measurable success criteria.

Design

Map journeys, create information architecture, wireframes and prototypes, then work closely with engineering.

Validate

Test the highest-risk assumptions with users or stakeholders. Track task completion, friction, feedback and errors.

Iterate

Use evidence to improve the design rather than defending the first idea.

Ship & learn

Handoff clearly, monitor real product signals and continue improving after launch.
